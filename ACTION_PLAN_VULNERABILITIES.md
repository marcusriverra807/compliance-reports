# Action Plan on Vulnerabilities

## Overview
This document outlines the action plan to address identified vulnerabilities in the system.

## Steps to Mitigate Vulnerabilities
1. **Identify Vulnerabilities**: Conduct regular scans to identify vulnerabilities.
2. **Assess Impact**: Evaluate the potential impact of each vulnerability.
3. **Prioritize Remediation**: Prioritize vulnerabilities based on impact and exploitability.
4. **Implement Fixes**: Apply patches and updates to mitigate vulnerabilities.
5. **Monitor**: Continuously monitor for new vulnerabilities and assess the effectiveness of fixes.

## Conclusion
Regular updates and monitoring are essential to maintain security and mitigate risks.
